## 1. Economy Sinks & Inflow Rules (standard §6)
Phase 3 માં કોઈ નવા currency કે pricing બદલાતા નથી. બધા નંબરો Shared Core Standard §6 ના જ લાગુ પડે છે:

### 1.1 Inflow (કમાણી)
- **Level First Clear:**
  - Easy (x5 Breathers): **15 coins**
  - Normal (x1, x2–x4, x6–x8, x0): **20 coins**
  - Hard (x9 Hard levels): **30 coins**
  - Phase Finale (L1500): **50 coins**
- **First ★★★ Bonus:** **+20 coins** (level દીઠ માત્ર 1 વાર).
- **2X Win (Rewarded Ad):** Base clear coins × 2 (Star bonus double થતો નથી).
- **Jungle Gift Basket (દરેક x0 milestone):**
  - Levels 1010, 1020, 1030 ... 1490 (કુલ 49 Baskets).
  - કિંમત: **300 coins + 1 booster** (Hint, Shield, Hammer ક્રમશઃ cycling).
- **Phase 3 Finale Chest (L1500):**
  - **1,000 coins** + 2 Glow Firefly Hint + 2 Safety Shell Shield + 2 Magic Stone Hammer + **Phase 3 Master Badge**.
- **Golden Slither Rush (Bonus Round):**
  - L1025, 1050, 1075 ... L1500 (કુલ 20 rounds).
  - 30 seconds, 30 snakes (Phase 2/3 tier max), 10 coins per snake + 100 coins full clear bonus (max 400 coins/run).

### 1.2 Outflow (ખર્ચ)
- **Boosters:**
  - Glow Firefly Hint: 60 coins (single) / 150 coins (3-pack).
  - Safety Shell Shield: 80 coins / 200 coins (3-pack).
  - Magic Stone Hammer: 100 coins / 250 coins (3-pack).
- **Heart Berry Refill:** 120 coins → +1 Heart (Second Chance popup માં, max 2 per attempt).
- **Streak Saver:** 200 coins.
- **Cosmetics:** Jungle skins & board themes (800 – 4,000 coins).
