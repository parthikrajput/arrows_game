## 1. Principles (સિદ્ધાંતો)
1. **50 Blocks × 10 Levels = 500 Levels:** Levels 3501 થી 4000.
2. **Fixed Difficulty Plateau (D 60–76):** પઝલની મુશ્કેલી વેલોની ઝડપી વૃદ્ધિ અને કાપણીના સમયબદ્ધ ક્રમથી આવે છે.
3. **Slot Mapping Rule (standard §17.38):**
   - **Block 1 x1 (L3501):** Signature System Intro (Overgrown Garden & Growth Rules).
   - **Block 2 x1 (L3511):** Base Mechanic 1 — M351 Wild Vine.
   - **Block 14 x1 (L3631):** Base Mechanic 2 — M352 Pruning Shears Tile.
   - **Block 26 x1 (L3751):** Base Mechanic 3 — M353 Seed-Carrier Snake.
   - **Block 38 x1 (L3871):** Base Mechanic 4 — M354 Bloom Gate.
   - **45 Variant Blocks:** x1 slots of blocks 3–13, 15–25, 27–37, 39–49.
   - **Block 50 x1 (L3991):** Review Level / "Mastery Mix" (કોઈ નવું item નહીં).
   - **Block 50 x0 (L4000):** Phase 8 Finale ("Heart of the Overgrowth") + Jungle Treasure Chest.
