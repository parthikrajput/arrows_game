## 2. Key Level Highlights

- **Level 9001 (Phase Opener):** Introduces M901 Wind-Driven Vine. Gentle 6x6 board where north wind forces vine growth into a single harmless corridor.
- **Level 9025 (Golden Slither Rush #1):** Fast-paced 25-turn coin dash through gentle river currents.
- **Level 9481–9490 (Block 949 Review):** 10 continuous multi-mechanic puzzle challenges where wind, river, rhythm, and echo must be calibrated in harmonious sequence.
- **Level 9500 (Apex of the Wild Currents — Grand Finale):** Massive 11x11 board. Dynamic monsoon storm wind pushes across split river mirrors while player maneuvers heavy python across rhythm echo locks to unlock the Sanctum of Currents. Difficulty $D = 91$.
