## 1. Economy Sinks & Inflow Rules (standard §6)
Phase 6 માં Shared Core Standard §6 ના સત્તાવાર નંબરો જ જળવાઈ રહે છે:

### 1.1 Inflow (કમાણી)
- **Level First Clear:**
  - Easy (x5 Breathers): **15 coins**
  - Normal (x1, x2–x4, x6–x8, x0): **20 coins**
  - Hard (x9 Hard levels): **30 coins**
  - Phase Finale (L3000): **50 coins**
- **First ★★★ Bonus:** **+20 coins** (level દીઠ 1 વાર).
- **2X Win (Rewarded Ad):** Base clear coins × 2.
- **Jungle Gift Basket (દરેક x0 milestone):**
  - Levels 2510, 2520 ... 2990 (કુલ 49 Baskets).
  - કિંમત: **300 coins + 1 booster** (Hint, Shield, Hammer ક્રમશઃ cycling).
- **Phase 6 Finale Chest (L3000):**
  - **1,000 coins** + 2 Glow Firefly Hint + 2 Safety Shell Shield + 2 Magic Stone Hammer + **Phase 6 Master Badge**.
- **Golden Slither Rush (Bonus Round):**
  - L2525, 2550, 2575 ... L3000 (કુલ 20 rounds).
  - 30 seconds, 30 snakes, 10 coins per snake + 100 coins full clear bonus (max 400 coins/run).

### 1.2 Outflow (ખર્ચ)
- **Boosters:** Hint (60 / 150), Shield (80 / 200), Hammer (100 / 250).
- **Heart Berry Refill:** 120 coins → +1 Heart.
- **Streak Saver:** 200 coins.
- **Cosmetics Shop:** Sun Temple Grand Board Theme & Prismatic Glow Snake Skin.
