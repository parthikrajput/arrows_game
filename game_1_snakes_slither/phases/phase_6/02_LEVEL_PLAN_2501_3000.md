## 1. Principles (સિદ્ધાંતો)
1. **50 Blocks × 10 Levels = 500 Levels:** Levels 2501 થી 3000.
2. **Fixed Difficulty Plateau (D 60–76):** પઝલની મુશ્કેલી ઓપ્ટિક્સ લોજિક અને પાથ ડિપેન્ડન્સી ચેઇન્સથી આવે છે.
3. **Slot Mapping Rule (standard §17.38):**
   - **Block 1 x1 (L2501):** Signature System Intro (Sun Temple Light & Filters).
   - **Block 2 x1 (L2511):** Base Mechanic 1 — M251 Petal Filter.
   - **Block 14 x1 (L2631):** Base Mechanic 2 — M252 Prism Lock.
   - **Block 26 x1 (L2751):** Base Mechanic 3 — M253 Sundial Receiver.
   - **Block 38 x1 (L2871):** Base Mechanic 4 — M254 Shade Leaves.
   - **45 Variant Blocks:** x1 slots of blocks 3–13, 15–25, 27–37, 39–49.
   - **Block 50 x1 (L2991):** Review Level / "Mastery Mix" (કોઈ નવું item નહીં).
   - **Block 50 x0 (L3000):** Phase 6 Finale ("Sun Temple Grand Sanctum") + Jungle Treasure Chest.
