# Card

A raised panel that groups one idea: the Daily card on Home, a shop tile, a settings group.

- `canopy`, radius `radius-lg`, padding `space-16`, shadow `elev-1`. No borders, no coloured side stripes.
- Title `h2` in `mist`; meta in `body` at 14px in `fern`; a reward line in `gold`.
- Leading icon sits in a 44px `moss` tile. Progress bars: 8px, `moss` track, `gold` fill.
- A whole card may be tappable; then it gets the `tap` sound and a pressed state in `moss`.
