# BoardSample

How the puzzle board sits on the dusk ground.

- Frame `board-well`, radius `radius-lg`; tiles `board` with corner radius 0.18 × cell and a 4px gutter.
- Snakes keep their 8 palette colours (`snake-*`) — they are the brightest things on screen, so nothing else on the board may use them. Each has a soft top highlight; the head is wider than the body and its eyes look the way it will move.
- Tongue-flick preview: dotted lane in `glow` when clear, `ember` with an ✕ on the blocker when blocked.
- Garden Rock: a rounded stone in the hammer's stone tones.
