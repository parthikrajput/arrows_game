# HudPill

Rounded status pills for the top of the screen: coins, heart berries, level, and small chips.

- Pill: `canopy`, height 40, radius `radius-pill`, numbers in `hud-number` with tabular figures in `mist`.
- Coins: `coin` icon 32px + amount + a small emerald `+` that opens the Shop.
- Hearts: three `heart` icons; a lost heart drops to 22% opacity and greyscale (never just a colour change). An active Shield shows the `shield` icon and its charges in `lagoon` beside them.
- Chips (height 28, `moss`): Moves, Hard (`ember` with `night` text), NEW (`glow` with `night` text), VIP (`orchid` with `night` text).
- Consumer provides: the numbers and which hearts are lost; tapping the coin pill is optional.
