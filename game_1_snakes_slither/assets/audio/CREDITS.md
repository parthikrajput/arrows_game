# Audio Asset Credits & Licenses

- All Sound Effects (SFX) are either custom-synthesized in-house or sourced under **Creative Commons 0 (CC0 / Public Domain)**.
- Music loops are original compositions or licensed royalty-free for commercial use.
- Total asset size budget: strictly <= 10 MB.
- Format: PCM 16-bit mono WAV for SFX, AAC-LC 128 kbps stereo M4A for music.
