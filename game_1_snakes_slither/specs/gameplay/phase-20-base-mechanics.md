## 2. Detailed Mechanic Specifications

### 2.1 M951: Apex Chieftain Mongoose (Grand Boss)
- **Level Intro:** Level 9511 (Block 952, x1 slot).
- **Behavior & Rules:**
  - $2 \times 2$ footprint apex predator patrolling defined routes.
  - Line-of-sight detection triggers if any snake segment rests within 4 cardinal tiles along facing direction.
  - **Telegraph Turn:** Boss roars, displaying glowing red claw markings across target tiles. Boss does NOT move during telegraph.
  - **Strike Action:** On subsequent turn, boss rushes 3 tiles forward.
  - **Baiting & Stun:** If player moves snake out of way, causing boss to collide with a wall, boulder, or trapdoor, boss is stunned for 3 turns.

### 2.2 M952: Prismatic Master Key (Omni Key)
- **Level Intro:** Level 9631 (Block 964, x1 slot).
- **Behavior & Rules:**
  - Floating crystalline key radiating prismatic light.
  - Collecting it triggers a board-wide chime, immediately opening all locked doors (ice seals, vine barriers, stone sluices, drum gates).
  - Streamlines final-tier puzzles by converting fragmented multi-key routing into master path convergence.

### 2.3 M953: Convergence Vortex (Dimensional Portal)
- **Level Intro:** Level 9751 (Block 976, x1 slot).
- **Behavior & Rules:**
  - Swirling cosmic vortex tile on grid.
  - When snake head enters, it immediately emerges from linked exit vortex with seamless tail curvature.
  - Maintains momentum and direction of entry vector.

### 2.4 M954: Jungle Crown Pedestal (Win Goal)
- **Level Intro:** Level 9871 (Block 988, x1 slot).
- **Behavior & Rules:**
  - Golden sovereign throne occupying center or pinnacle of board.
  - Level victory requires navigating the designated golden serpent head onto the pedestal.
  - Once mounted, triggers the Level 10,000 Grand Victory coronation sequence.
