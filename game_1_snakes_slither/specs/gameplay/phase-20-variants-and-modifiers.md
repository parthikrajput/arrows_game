## 1. Modifier Catalog (MOD-01 through MOD-10)

Phase 20 applies 10 systematic modifiers to the 4 Grand Master mechanics across 45 unique variants:
- **MOD-01 (Multi-Stage):** 3-phase boss battles, multi-ring vortex networks.
- **MOD-02 (Directional / Polarized):** Strict cardinal attack angles, polarized vortex gates.
- **MOD-03 (Color-Coded):** Color-matched crown pedestals and prismatic key fragments.
- **MOD-04 (Move Countdown):** Enraged boss timer, decaying omni-key radiance.
- **MOD-05 (Speed / Rapid Charge):** Double-speed boss rush and high-velocity vortex ejectors.
- **MOD-06 (Inverted Vortex):** Vortex reversing snake head-to-tail direction.
- **MOD-07 (Paired Bosses):** Two mongoose chieftains coordinating pincer rushes.
- **MOD-08 (Rotating Crown Dais):** Crown pedestal rotating access steps every turn.
- **MOD-09 (Ice Slide Boss Arena):** Boss encounter situated on frozen slippery rink.
- **MOD-10 (Obstacle Guarded):** Iron portcullises and bottomless chasms surrounding crown.
