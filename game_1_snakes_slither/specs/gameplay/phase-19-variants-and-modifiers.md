## 1. Modifier Catalog (MOD-01 through MOD-10)

Phase 19 applies 10 systematic modifiers to the 4 synergy mechanics across 45 unique variants:
- **MOD-01 (Multi-Stage):** 3-stage vine growth branching, triple-river cascades.
- **MOD-02 (Directional / Polarized):** One-way wind corridors, polarized siphon sluices.
- **MOD-03 (Color-Coded):** Color-tuned echo bells, colored reflection lilies.
- **MOD-04 (Move Countdown):** Rapidly decaying seismic vibrations, decaying water levels.
- **MOD-05 (Speed / Rapid Flow):** High-velocity torrent carrying snakes 2 tiles per current tick.
- **MOD-06 (Inverted Cycle):** Inverted drum downbeat reversing wind direction.
- **MOD-07 (Paired Rivers):** Synchronously linked waterways with shared hydraulic locks.
- **MOD-08 (Rotating Currents):** Siphon river exit pivoting $90^\circ$ each turn.
- **MOD-09 (Ice Slide Synergy):** Frozen banks propelling vines or heavy pythons onto pressure points.
- **MOD-10 (Obstacle Guarded):** Dense granite pillars and thorn barriers shielding seismic bells.
