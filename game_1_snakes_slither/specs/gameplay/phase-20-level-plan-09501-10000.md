## 1. Phase Structure & Sub-Tiers

Phase 20 spans the final 500 levels of the game across 50 discrete blocks (Blocks 951 to 1000):
- **Introductory Ascent (Blocks 951–960, Levels 9501–9600):** $D = 70 \rightarrow 74$. Introduction of M951 Chieftain Mongoose Boss, M952 Prismatic Master Key, M953 Convergence Vortex, and M954 Crown Pedestal.
- **High-Plateau Trajectory (Blocks 961–998, Levels 9601–9980):** High plateau oscillating between $D = 74$ and $D = 82$. Confluence of all 14 signature systems.
- **Master Gauntlet (Block 999, Levels 9981–9990):** $D = 80 \rightarrow 85$. Comprehensive review of all 14 phase signature mechanics.
- **The Summit Run (Block 1000, Levels 9991–9999):** $D = 86 \rightarrow 92$. Intense multi-layer puzzles leading directly to the summit.
- **Level 10,000 Grand Finale ("Jungle Kingdom Crown"):** $D = 95$. The ultimate climax of the entire 10,000-level journey on a $14 \times 14$ master grid.

## 2. 10-Level Block Template

Every 10-level block adheres strictly to the canonical distribution:
- **Slot 1 (x1):** Mechanic/Variant Introduction (Easy, $D = 70$).
- **Slot 2 (x2):** Reinforcement & Basic Routing (Easy, $D = 72$).
- **Slot 3 (x3):** Extended Path & Squeeze Navigation (Normal, $D = 75$).
- **Slot 4 (x4):** Recovery & Rhythm Check (Easy, $D = 71$).
- **Slot 5 (x5):** Multi-Snake Synchronization (Normal, $D = 78$).
- **Slot 6 (x6):** Density Spike & Narrow Channels (Normal, $D = 80$).
- **Slot 7 (x7):** Boss Encounter / Dynamic Shift Testing (Normal, $D = 82$).
- **Slot 8 (x8):** Pre-Climax Pressure (Normal, $D = 84$).
- **Slot 9 (x9):** Block Climax (Hard, $D \approx 85-88$).
- **Slot 10 (x0):** Milestone Gate & Gift Basket Unlock (Milestone, $D = 80$).

## 4. Finale Level 10,000 Technical Parameters
- **Level Name:** Jungle Kingdom Crown.
- **Grid Dimension:** $14 \times 14$ master arena with mandatory pan and pinch zoom.
- **Snake Count:** 42 snakes (8 length-8, 14 length-6, 12 length-4, 8 length-3).
- **Obstacles:** 15 obstacles (sanctuary pillars, ice rinks, conveyor flumes, drum gates, vine trellises).
- **Active Boss:** Prowling Mongoose Emperor (Apex Boss M951).
- **Objective:** Evade/stun Emperor, retrieve Prismatic Master Key (M952), traverse Convergence Vortex (M953), and seat the Sovereign Serpent on the Jungle Crown Pedestal (M954).
- **Estimated Solver Move Budget:** 88 moves ($D = 95$).
