## 1. Modifier Catalog (MOD-01 through MOD-10)

Phase 18 applies 10 systematic modifiers to the 4 synergy mechanics across 45 unique variants:
- **MOD-01 (Multi-Stage):** 3-floor elevator cascades, multi-stage boulder rolling.
- **MOD-02 (Directional / Polarized):** One-way elevator ramps and polarized light refractors.
- **MOD-03 (Color-Coded):** Color-matched solar portals accessible only by identical colored snakes.
- **MOD-04 (Move Countdown):** Rapidly decaying cycle dials or timed solar shutters.
- **MOD-05 (Speed / Rapid Synergy):** High-velocity rolling coconut launching snakes upon impact.
- **MOD-06 (Inverted Cycle):** Inverted day/night phase reversing elevator elevation rules.
- **MOD-07 (Paired Mirrors):** Synchronized dual prisms splitting solar beams.
- **MOD-08 (Rotating Prisms):** Prism reflectors revolving $90^\circ$ every 3 moves.
- **MOD-09 (Ice Slide Synergy):** Boulder and portal layouts situated on frozen ice rinks.
- **MOD-10 (Obstacle Guarded):** Dense stone and thorny barricades shielding pressure plates.
