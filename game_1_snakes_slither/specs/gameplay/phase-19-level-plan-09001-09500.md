## 1. Phase Structure & Sub-Tiers

Phase 19 spans 500 levels structured across 50 discrete blocks (Blocks 901 to 950) divided into two sub-tiers:
- **Sub-Tier P19-A (Levels 9001–9250, Blocks 901–925):** Focus on M901 Wind-Driven Vine and M902 Split River Bridge. Difficulty plateau $D \in [60, 72]$.
- **Sub-Tier P19-B (Levels 9251–9500, Blocks 926–950):** Focus on M903 Rhythm Monsoon Cascade, M904 Echo Weight Shift, and 4-way late-era synergy. Difficulty plateau $D \in [68, 76]$; Hard slots $D \approx 84$; Review Block 949 $D \approx 78$; Grand Finale Level 9500 "Apex of the Wild Currents" $D \approx 91$.

## 2. 10-Level Block Template

Every 10-level block adheres strictly to the canonical distribution:
- **Slot 1 (x1):** Mechanic/Variant Introduction (Easy, $D = 60$).
- **Slot 2 (x2):** Reinforcement & Basic Routing (Easy, $D = 62$).
- **Slot 3 (x3):** Extended Path & Squeeze Navigation (Normal, $D = 65$).
- **Slot 4 (x4):** Recovery & Rhythm Check (Easy, $D = 61$).
- **Slot 5 (x5):** Multi-Snake Synchronization (Normal, $D = 68$).
- **Slot 6 (x6):** Density Spike & Narrow Channels (Normal, $D = 71$).
- **Slot 7 (x7):** Synergy Reversal / Dynamic Shift Testing (Normal, $D = 73$).
- **Slot 8 (x8):** Pre-Climax Pressure (Normal, $D = 75$).
- **Slot 9 (x9):** Block Climax (Hard, $D \approx 82-85$).
- **Slot 10 (x0):** Milestone Gate & Gift Basket Unlock (Milestone, $D = 74$).

## 4. Finale Level 9500 Technical Parameters
- **Level Name:** Apex of the Wild Currents.
- **Grid Dimension:** $14 \times 14$ with pan and pinch zoom enabled.
- **Snake Count:** 42 snakes (8 length-8, 14 length-6, 12 length-4, 8 length-3).
- **Obstacles:** 15 obstacles (storm pillars, siphon gates, seismic resonant bells).
- **Active Mechanics:** M901 + M902 + M903 + M904 full integration.
- **Estimated Solver Move Budget:** 76 moves ($D \approx 91$).
