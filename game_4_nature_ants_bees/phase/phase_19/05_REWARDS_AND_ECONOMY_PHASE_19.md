## 1. Economy Numbers (Standard §6 મુજબ)
- **Level First Clear:**
  - Easy (Breather x5): **15 coins**
  - Normal (x1–x4, x6–x8, x0): **20 coins**
  - Hard (x9): **30 coins**
  - Phase Finale (L9500): **50 coins**
  - First ★★★ Clear Bonus: **+20 coins**
  - Replays: **0 coins**
- **Queen's Gift Box (દરેક x0 Milestone Level):**
  - Levels 9010, 9020 … 9490 (49 Gift Boxes).
  - Reward: **300 coins + 1 rotating booster** (`(L/10) mod 3`: 1 = Nectar Glow Hint, 2 = Leaf Blower, 0 = Queen's Royal Shield).
- **Royal Honey Chest (Level 9500 Finale):**
  - **1,000 coins**
  - **2 Nectar Glow Hints + 2 Queen's Royal Shields + 2 Leaf Blowers**
  - **"Master Composer"** cosmetic profile badge.
- **Golden Nectar Rush:**
  - દર 25 levels એ અનલૉક થાય છે (L9025, L9050, ..., L9500).
  - 30 સેકન્ડ્સમાં 30 lines નો frenzied board: 10 coins per line + 100 completion bonus.
