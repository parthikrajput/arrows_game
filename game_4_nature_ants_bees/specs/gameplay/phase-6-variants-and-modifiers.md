# Spec: Gameplay — Phase 6 Variants & Modifiers

## Status
Draft

## Type
New Feature

## Goal
Specify the 45 variant slots in Phase 6 (Levels 2501–3000) generated by combining Phase 6 optical base mechanics (M251–M254) and previous obstacles with the 10 standard modifiers, following the Standard §11 composition formula.

## Current State
Design documented in `phase/phase_6/04_VARIANTS_AND_MODIFIERS_PHASE_6.md` and `SCALING_TO_10000_LEVELS.md` §7, not yet implemented.
- Previous phases combined modifiers with static, multi-floor, and push obstacles.
- Applying modifiers to optical elements (petal filters, twin receptors, sundial timers, shade sectors) is introduced in Phase 6.

## Proposed Change
Implement optical modifier bindings in `packages/puzzle_core` across Blocks 3–49 (excluding intro blocks 14, 26, 38):

### 1. Optical Modifier Interaction Rules
- `Frozen(K)`: Petal filter, prism, or sundial lens remains frozen/opaque until $K$ adjacent lines exit.
- `Colored(c)`: Restricts interaction or filtering specifically to color $c$.
- `Fogged`: Optical receptor or shade patch is shrouded under M010 fog until revealed.
- `Chained`: Two petal filters or twin-sun gates are tethered; state shifts on one instantly mirror to the other.
- `Countdown(N)`: Sundial lens or filter dissolves after $N$ player Moves.
- `Phasing`: Filter or receptor toggles active on Even Moves and intangible on Odd Moves.
- `Armored`: Structural optical mounts immune to Leaf Blower.
- `Layered(mask)`: Restricts optical filtering or receiver sensitivity strictly to specified layer mask.
- `Mirrored`: Symmetrically placed prisms or filters across board axis.
- `Growing(N)`: Brambles spread across optical lines of sight every $N$ moves.
