# Spec: Gameplay — Phase 7 Variants & Modifiers

## Status
Draft

## Type
New Feature

## Goal
Specify the 45 variant slots in Phase 7 (Levels 3001–3500) generated by combining Phase 7 rotation base mechanics (M301–M304) and previous obstacles with the 10 standard modifiers, following the Standard §11 composition formula.

## Current State
Design documented in `phase/phase_7/04_VARIANTS_AND_MODIFIERS_PHASE_7.md` and `SCALING_TO_10000_LEVELS.md` §7, not yet implemented.
- Previous phases applied modifiers to static, multi-floor, push, and optical elements.
- Applying modifiers to sub-board rotating platforms, shifting perimeter edges, and auto-spinning lines is introduced in Phase 7.

## Proposed Change
Implement rotational modifier bindings in `packages/puzzle_core` across Blocks 3–49 (excluding intro blocks 14, 26, 38):

### 1. Rotational Modifier Interaction Rules
- `Frozen(K)`: Turntable button or spinning line remains frozen until $K$ adjacent lines exit.
- `Colored(c)`: Restricts rotation or interaction strictly to lines of matching color $c$.
- `Fogged`: Turntable center or rotating edge gap is shrouded under fog until cleared.
- `Chained`: Two turntables are linked; rotating one rotates the second $90^\circ$ simultaneously.
- `Countdown(N)`: Turntable can only be rotated $N$ times before permanently seizing.
- `Phasing`: Perimeter hedge shifts presence on alternate moves.
- `Armored`: Turntable mechanism or pinned line cannot be removed by Leaf Blower.
- `Layered(mask)`: Restricts rotation effect strictly to worker bees (`[1]`) or ground ants (`[0]`).
- `Mirrored`: Symmetrical turntables placed on opposing board halves.
- `Growing(N)`: Brambles spread across turntable cells every $N$ moves.
