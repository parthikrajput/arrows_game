# Spec: Gameplay — Phase 4 Variants & Modifiers

## Status
Draft

## Type
New Feature

## Goal
Specify the 45 variant slots in Phase 4 (Levels 1501–2000) generated by combining Phase 4 multi-floor base mechanics (M151–M154) and previous obstacles with the 10 standard modifiers, following the Standard §11 composition formula.

## Current State
Design documented in `phase/phase_4/04_VARIANTS_AND_MODIFIERS_PHASE_4.md` and `SCALING_TO_10000_LEVELS.md` §7, not yet implemented.
- Phase 3 established the standard 10 modifiers on single-floor boards.
- Applying modifiers to vertical multi-floor mechanics (stairs, transparent glass, drop holes, lifts) is introduced in Phase 4.

## Proposed Change
Implement multi-floor modifier bindings in `packages/puzzle_core` across Blocks 3–49 (excluding intro blocks 14, 26, 38):

### 1. Multi-Floor Modifier Interaction Rules
- `Frozen(K)`: Stairs or lifts remain locked until $K$ lines on the same floor successfully exit.
- `Colored(c)`: Only lines of color $c$ can enter the stairs/lift; other colors treat it as impassable or flat ground.
- `Fogged`: Inter-floor portal or glass tile is hidden under fog until cleared by adjacent line movement.
- `Chained`: Linked lifts or stairs operate in tandem across multiple floors.
- `Countdown(N)`: Stairs or hole remains open for $N$ moves, then permanently collapses/seals.
- `Phasing`: Stairs shift presence between floors on alternate moves.
- `Armored`: Immune to Leaf Blower; structural multi-floor elements.
- `Layered(mask)`: Restricts stairs or lift specifically to worker bees (`[1]`) or ants (`[0]`).
- `Mirrored`: Symmetrically placed stairs on opposite halves of the terrace.
- `Growing(N)`: Brambles expand across terrace edges every $N$ moves.
