# Bots Mode Test Report: Levels 1–500

Date: 2026-11-01 · Build: branch `snakes-slither` (uncommitted Bots mode work)

## How the test was run

- The real app ran in a phone-sized test window (412 × 915), with animations on.
- The bot started at Level 1 in Bots mode. After each win it moved on to the next level on its own, just as a tester sees on a device.
- The bot taps the board the way a player does, so every tap goes through the real game rules, hearts, moves and win screen.
- For each level, the run recorded the steps the stored solution needs, the steps the bot completed, any step where it left the solution, hearts lost, stars, any screen error, and any stall (no move for 30 s, or the bot stopping itself).
- To repeat it: `BOTS_ALL=1 flutter test test/bots_all_levels_test.dart` (from `game_1_snakes_slither/`). The raw data goes to `build/bots_report.json`.

## Issues found and fixed during this work

Each one was found while testing Bots mode, fixed, then checked again by the full run.

1. **Bot used a step counter, not the board.** After Restart, or after the player made their own moves, the bot replayed the wrong steps. It now reads the board each turn: it follows the stored solution while the moves still match it, and otherwise works out the next move the same way Hint does.
2. **Bot wins saved progress and paid coins.** Opening Level 300 with the bot and winning set "highest cleared" to 300, which unlocked the whole map, and paid coins and gift baskets. Bot runs now leave progress, coins, tutorial boosters and analytics untouched.
3. **Locked levels could be opened without Bots mode.** The map's lock check had been removed. It is back: only the bot can open a locked level, and a locked level's card shows just the Bot button.
4. **Bot stopped after one level.** "Next" dropped Bots mode. A bot win now moves on to the next level with the bot still on, and returns to the map after Level 500.
5. **Bot could stall silently.** A refused tap or a board with no way out left the bot waiting forever. It now stops and says why on its chip ("Stopped: move N was refused", "no way out from here", "out of hearts").
6. **Tutorial cards blocked the board in bot runs.** Tips and "NEW!" cards are now skipped in bot runs. They still show for players.
7. **Level card text overflowed** on phone width ("3 hearts · N moves par · tap snakes out" + "D x.x"). The text now wraps.
8. **Bots mode was in release builds.** It now shows only in debug and profile builds.
