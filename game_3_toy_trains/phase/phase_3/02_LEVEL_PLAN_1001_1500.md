## 3. Tiers & Difficulty Progression
- **Tier P3-A (L1001–1250):** Grid 12×12 to 13×13, 28–34 trains, D 60.0–68.0.
- **Tier P3-B (L1251–1500):** Grid 13×13 to 14×14, 32–40 trains, D 68.0–76.0.
- **Plateau Rule:** Difficulty remains strictly bounded within the D 60–76 window, providing sustained intellectual depth without mechanical unfairness.
