## 3. Tiers & Difficulty Progression
- **Tier P5-A (L2001–2250):** Single Carts & Pits, 28–34 trains, D 60.0–68.0.
- **Tier P5-B (L2251–2500):** Multi-Cart chains & Heavy Ballast, 32–40 trains, D 68.0–76.0.
- **Plateau Rule:** Difficulty remains strictly bounded within the D 60–76 window.
