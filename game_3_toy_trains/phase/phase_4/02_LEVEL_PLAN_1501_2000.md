## 3. Tiers & Difficulty Progression
- **Tier P4-A (L1501–1750):** 2 Floors (F1/F2), 28–34 trains, D 60.0–68.0.
- **Tier P4-B (L1751–2000):** 3 Floors (F1/F2/F3), 32–40 trains, D 68.0–76.0.
- **Plateau Rule:** Difficulty remains strictly bounded within the D 60–76 window.
