## 1. 50-Block Architecture
Phase 4 દરેક 10 levels નો એક block ધરાવે છે (L1501 થી L2000):
- **Block 1 (L1501–1510):** Multi-Floor Intro (L1501 — 2 floors, switcher buttons introduction).
- **Block 2 (L1511–1520):** Base Mechanic 1 Intro — M151 Spiral Spool Stair (L1511).
- **Block 14 (L1631–1640):** Base Mechanic 2 Intro — M152 Organza Floor (L1631).
- **Block 26 (L1751–1760):** Base Mechanic 3 Intro — M153 Drop Hole (L1751).
- **Block 38 (L1871–1880):** Base Mechanic 4 Intro — M154 Bobbin Lift (L1871).
- **45 Variant Slots:** બાકીના 45 blocks ના x1 slots પર M151–M154 × 10 craft modifiers ના સંયોજનો.
- **Block 50 (L1991–2000):** L1991 = Review Level ("Multi-Floor Mastery"); L2000 = Phase Finale "The Three-Tier Quilt".

## 4. Finale Level 2000 ("The Three-Tier Quilt")
- **Layout:** 3 સંપૂર્ણ ફ્લોર્સ (14×14 દરેક), જેમાં Spiral Spool Stairs, Organza Sections, Drop Holes, Bobbin Lifts અને ત્રણેય ફ્લોર્સ પર પથરાયેલા 40 દોરાઓ સામેલ છે.
- **Target D:** 96.0 (CI pre-validated, 0 hearts & 0 boosters solvable).
- **Rewards:** Unlocks Phase 4 Master Badge, 1,000 Coins, 2 of each booster, followed by L2000 Golden Bobbin Rush.
