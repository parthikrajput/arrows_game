## 2. Total Phase 7 Coins Potential
- **Base Level Clears (500 levels):** ~11,000 Coins.
- **First ★★★ Bonus (500 levels):** 10,000 Coins.
- **49 Craft Baskets:** 14,700 Coins + 49 Boosters (17 Hints, 16 Hammers, 16 Shields).
- **20 Golden Bobbin Rush Rounds:** ~7,000 Coins.
- **Grand Sewing Chest (L3500):** 1,000 Coins + 6 Boosters.
- **કુલ Phase 7 ઇનફ્લો:** $\approx 43,700$ Coins + 55 Boosters.

## 3. Sinks & Invariants
- **Booster Prices (અપરિવર્તિત):** Needle Glow Hint (60 / 150), Thimble Shield (80 / 200), Golden Scissors (100 / 250).
- **Heart Refill:** 120 Coins (માત્ર Second Chance માં, attempt દીઠ મહત્તમ 2).
- **Darning Patch:** 200 Coins અથવા 1 rewarded ad.
- **Deferral Rule:** Craft Basket, Coin Rush અથવા Daily Stitch જીત પછી Interstitial ad ક્યારેય તરત દેખાતી નથી; આગલી eligible સામાન્ય જીત પર defer થાય છે.
